## Description

Four in a row on any timeline wins. Drop discs, turn the board so gravity moves them, or send a disc into the past to branch history. Every waiting board needs an answer.

Play a three-level bot, share one device, or exchange game codes by message. Try nine puzzles, watch replays and explore optional pop-out, flip and strict-present rules. Pick five board skins and four piece sets in light or dark mode. All cosmetic extras are available free while billing is disabled.
